## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **AI2U: With You 'Til The End**. This trainer adds optional quality-of-life controls for AI2U: With You 'Til The End. It can protect the player from damage, improve movement and exploration, manage trust and survival values, preserve useful inventory items, and simplify puzzle progression without changing the game's AI dialogue system.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore every level, experiment with AI-driven conversations, unlock alternate endings, revisit puzzles, or reduce repetitive survival and inventory management.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Invincibility** — Prevents damage from environmental dangers, hostile encounters, and scripted threats.
- **Freeze Threats** — Pauses selected hostile or pursuing encounters while leaving exploration and dialogue available.
- **Quick Interaction** — Speeds up selected interaction and transition delays during item collection, puzzle use, and environmental exploration.

### 📌 Survival

- **Unlimited Health** — Keeps the player's health from decreasing during exploration and dangerous sequences.

### 📌 Dialogue

- **Maximum Trust** — Sets the companion relationship value to its highest practical level for easier cooperation and alternate endings.
- **Trust Lock** — Prevents trust from decreasing when the player ignores requests, explores independently, or makes unfavorable choices.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Consumables** — Prevents potions and other usable items from being consumed permanently.
- **Inventory Protection** — Keeps collected quest items, puzzle pieces, ingredients, gifts, and other important objects available.
- **Item Duplication** — Duplicates selected inventory items for repeated puzzle attempts, crafting, or gift interactions.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Increased Movement Speed** — Adjusts walking and running speed to make apartments, cabins, space facilities, islands, and maze areas faster to traverse.
- **No Fall Damage** — Protects the player from damage caused by falls and risky movement through elevated areas.

### 📌 Puzzle

- **Puzzle Assist** — Provides optional assistance for item-placement puzzles, maze objectives, alchemy tasks, and other escape-room challenges.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game first, load a save, and wait until the player has full control. Activate movement-related options only after entering a level. Increased movement speed is useful for large environments and maze sections, while No Fall Damage protects against accidental drops. Restore normal speed before scripted chase sequences if character positioning becomes unreliable.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the game's inventory or quick inventory system before activating item options. Select the item you want to preserve or duplicate, then enable Inventory Protection or Item Duplication. These options are intended for puzzle pieces, ingredients, potions, gifts, and quest objects. Keep at least one original copy of important items and save before experimenting with story-critical objects.

### World & Environment

AI2U is structured around themed escape-room levels where players investigate environments, collect clues, interact with AI companions, solve puzzles, and pursue different endings. Use Puzzle Assist when a puzzle blocks progress, and use Freeze Threats or Invincibility when you want to examine an area without pressure. Save regularly before entering new rooms, anomaly areas, maze sections, or major story transitions.

### Combat & Weapons

The game is not built around conventional weapon-based combat. Dangerous encounters are handled through positioning, dialogue, puzzle progression, item use, escape sequences, and survival mechanics. Invincibility, Unlimited Health, and Freeze Threats are therefore the primary defensive options. They can reduce the consequences of hostile encounters without adding weapons or combat systems that are not part of the original game.
